The `CodeRabbit` check is advisory: its findings are suggestions to adopt where they improve the code, listed as a warning, never a reason to stop a commit. A review of the same diff words its findings differently on every run, so a record keyed by the wording could never be relied on to match the next review, and the check could not pass on a large change. A review that cannot be read warns too; a broken `.repo-contract/exceptions/coderabbit.json` still fails. On a pull request, CodeRabbit's GitHub App review and review-thread resolution are unchanged.
